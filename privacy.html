Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Peng Console is a personal tool used only by its developer. It is not offered to anyone else.

  • With the account owner's own consent, it reads and updates that owner's Google Calendar events, and reads subscription receipt emails in that owner's Gmail, only to show them on the owner's private dashboard.
  • Data is processed on servers controlled by the owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or model training.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
